# Structured SMC′ compact audit artifacts

See `docs/msrc_smc_structured_validation.md` for interpretation and commands.
The new model freezes the MSRC marginal at a declared reference coordinate;
it is not validated for position-dependent marginals over a whole chromosome.

- `marginal_tests.csv`: audited primary grid, 30 settings × 1,000 chromosomes
  per method; 315 comparisons in one Holm family.
- `refined_marginal_tests.csv`: three k=1 four-taxon settings expanded to 3,000
  replicates, including the primary first 1,000; not independent replication.
- `spatial_summary.csv`: chromosome bootstrap intervals for several site pairs;
  `topology_excess_agreement.csv` subtracts the independent marginal baseline.
- `off_reference_marginals.csv`: deliberate measurement of the frozen-reference
  bias away from coordinate 50.
- `path_summary.csv`, `background_tests.csv`, `background_rate_tests.csv`:
  marginal jump counts, total background dwell, and exposure-based rate audit.
  Rare-count practical intervals can remain unresolved.
- `waiting_summary.csv`, `tract_summary.csv`: completed and censored intervals
  distinguished; completed-only means are descriptive, not lifetime estimators.
- `temporal_tests.csv`, `histories.json`, `*_frequency_history.csv`: two actual
  shared Moran/WF paths, seed 93, 500 conditional replicates each.
- `window_spatial_summary.csv`: 500 replicates per k, nonuniform map and both
  suppression boundaries; see the report for exact settings.
- `benchmark.csv`, `profile.txt`: final analytic fast mode, one seeded run per
  workload. `benchmark_uncached_audit.csv` and `benchmark_cached_audit.csv`
  preserve the profiling/optimization history. These are not controlled
  replicated speed comparisons; marks differ between algorithms.
- `validation.png`, `off_reference.png`: marginal and dependence diagnostics.

Versions, seeds, test outcomes and scope are recorded in validation_summary.json.
Large raw draw/event/position tables are regenerated by the supplied scripts
and were written under /private/tmp for this audit. The example chromosome
and its exact paths/event records are retained in the sibling example folder.
